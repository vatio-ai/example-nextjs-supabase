# Shipping and returns

## How long does shipping take?

Orders are prepared within 1 business day, then shipped. Standard shipping
arrives 3 to 5 business days after the order ships, and express shipping 1 to
2 business days after it ships. An order placed on a weekend or a holiday is
prepared on the next business day.

## How much does shipping cost?

Standard shipping is free on orders over $50 and costs $5 below that. Express
shipping costs $15 on any order.

## What does each order status mean?

- **Processing**: the order is being prepared and has not shipped yet.
- **Shipped**: the order has left the warehouse and is on its way.
- **Delivered**: the carrier has delivered the order.
- **Cancelled**: the order was cancelled and will not ship. Anything charged
  for it is refunded.

## Can I cancel or change an order?

An order can be cancelled or changed while it is still processing. Once it has
shipped, it can no longer be cancelled; return it instead. Cancellations and
changes are done by the support team, not online.

## How do I return an item?

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery, unused and in their original
packaging. The support team sends a prepaid return label. Returns are free
when the item arrived damaged or was the wrong item; otherwise a $5 return
shipping fee is taken from the refund.

## When do I get my refund?

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of the
return arriving at the warehouse. Shipping costs are refunded only when the
item arrived damaged or was the wrong item.

## What if my order arrived damaged or never arrived?

Write to the support team within 14 days of delivery, or of the expected
delivery date if it never arrived. They send a replacement or a full refund,
including shipping.
